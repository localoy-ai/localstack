# Ads and performance video

An ad has one job: make the right person do one thing. Everything serves
the hook, the proof and the ask.

## The first second

- The first frame is the thumbnail and the hook: motion, a face, a bold
  claim or a pattern break. Never a logo or a slow fade-in.
- Name the viewer or their problem fast ("If you run a bakery…").

## Structures that work

- **Problem → agitate → solution** (PAS): the pain, why it's worse than
  they think, the product fixing it.
- **Before → after**: the same situation, with and without.
- **Demo**: the product doing the thing, start to finish, fast.
- **Testimonial / UGC**: a real person, their words, their result.
- **List**: "3 ways to…", each a beat, the product in one or all.
- **Offer**: the deal, why now, the ask; for retargeting.

## Proof

- Show, don't claim: the result on screen, a number, a review, a logo row.
- Claims need a source; never invent numbers, quotes or reviews.

## The ask

- One action, said and shown (button, address), in the last 2–3 s; for
  long ads, once in the middle too.

## Variants

- Make 2–4 versions that differ in one thing (the hook, usually) so the
  person can test them; same body, different first 3 seconds.
- Cut the same ad to each placement: 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts), 1:1 or
  4:5 (feeds), 16:9 (YouTube); 6 s, 15 s and 30 s.

## Craft

- Captions always; logo small in a corner, big only at the end.
- Brand colours and type in every text; one accent.
- Sound on every cut; music that starts with energy.

## What this means in VertX

- One project per placement (`new` with its preset); copy the edit by
  making the same calls; `export` each with a clear name (brand-hook-a-9x16).
