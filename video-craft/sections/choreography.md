# Choreography and movement on screen

Movement on screen is designed twice: once as the movement, once as how
the camera and the cut show it.

## Dance

- **Counts and phrases**: dancers think in 8-counts; a phrase is usually
  one or two 8-counts. Plan moves on the music's counts and cut on the
  downbeat (count 1) or on the move's accent.
- **Formations**: lines, V-shapes, circles, staggered rows; a change of
  formation is a scene change. The lead dancer centre or at the V's point.
- **Levels**: high (jumps, reaches), middle, low (floor work) — vary them.
- **Canon and unison**: everyone together for power; one after another
  (a ripple) for flow.
- **Showing it**: a wide for the formation, a medium for the move, a
  close-up for the face and the hands on the accent. Don't cut mid-move
  unless the next shot continues it.
- **Cultural forms**: name the form and its vocabulary (Kathak spins and
  footwork, Bharatanatyam mudras, Bihu, folk circle dances, hip-hop
  grooves) and respect it: costumes, gestures and music of the form.

## Fights and action

- **Tell a story in the fight**: who's winning, the turn, the cost.
- **Clear geography**: a wide early so we know where everyone is.
- **Action and reaction**: a strike and the hit land in different shots
  cut on the impact.
- **Speed is in the cut**, not only the movement.
- **Safety and taste**: no gore in short-form for general audiences; imply
  rather than show.

## Groups and crowds

- One or two clear leaders in the frame; the rest as texture.
- Move the group as a shape (a wave, a parting, a gathering).

## Movement in still-based video

Most of a VertX piece is stills with camera motion, so movement is
suggested:

- **Pose sequences**: 2–4 pictures of the same moment in consecutive poses
  (wind-up, strike, follow-through), cut fast (0.3–0.6 s each), read as
  motion.
- **Camera motion against implied motion**: a pan in the direction the
  character moves adds speed.
- **Match on action across stills**: the hand rising in one, the hand up in
  the next.
- **Real clips for the one move that must flow** (the spin, the leap),
  started from the same picture so the look holds.

## Syncing movement to music

- Mark the beats (and the bigger accents) of the music first; place cuts
  and pose changes on them.
- Slow sections: longer holds, pushes; fast sections: cuts on every beat or
  every other beat.

## What this means in VertX

- Pose sequences: several `picture`s with short `for` (0.3–0.6 s), same
  cast and style; or a `video` clip `from` the first pose for real motion.
- Cuts on the beat: place each clip's `at` on the music's beat times (no
  automatic beat detection yet).
- Can't do yet: speed ramps, motion blur, beat detection, multi-dancer
  sync in generated clips (keep groups small and clips short).
