# Formats: documentary, explainer, ad, adaptation, podcast

Each format has a shape people expect. Use it, then surprise inside it.

## Documentary and real stories

- **A question, not a topic.** "Why do Dhaka's tea stalls never close?"
  beats "Tea stalls of Dhaka".
- **Characters carry it**: one or two real people with something at stake.
- **Show, then tell**: a moment first, the explanation after.
- **Truth rules**: real quotes only, real places named honestly, nothing
  staged passed off as caught, AI pictures labelled as illustration when
  they stand in for real events.

## Explainer and tutorial

- **Start with the result**: show what they'll be able to do.
- **One step at a time**, each a scene: action, then what it does.
- **Name things once, the same way every time.**
- **Check understanding with a picture**: a diagram, a before/after, a
  zoom on the important part.
- **End with the next step** they can take now.

## Ads and brand pieces

- **One benefit you can see**, not a list of features.
- **Proof**: a demo, a real customer, a number they gave you (never
  invented).
- **Brand early but not first**: the product within the first few
  seconds, the logo can wait.
- **The offer and the ask**, plainly, at the end, on screen and in voice.
- **Follow DESIGN.md**: colours, fonts, words to avoid.

## Adapting a book, article or post

- **Find the spine**: the one through-line the video can carry; leave the
  rest.
- **Turn description into scenes**: what would we see?
- **Keep the author's best lines**, word for word, with credit.
- **Respect rights**: adapt what the person owns or has permission for.

## Podcast and audio shapes

| Shape | How it runs |
|---|---|
| Solo | Cold open line → intro → 2–4 segments → takeaway → outro |
| Interview | Cold open (the guest's best moment) → intro → warm-up → 3–5 question blocks → rapid fire → outro |
| Panel | Host frames the question → each voice → disagreement → host sums up |
| Narrative | Scene → narration → tape (quotes) → turn → next scene; a mystery or a journey |
| Audio drama | Scenes with sound: every place needs its sound, every action a sound cue |

For each: the episode's **one question or promise** up front, segment
timings in the outline, music stings between segments, and show notes
(summary, chapters with times, links).

## What this means in VertX

- Documentary and explainer: pictures with `motion`, `text` labels,
  `captions`; narration via `voice`, placed with `sync`.
- Ads: tight timings, `text` for the offer and the ask; check the logo and
  colours with `look`.
- Podcast: an audio-only project (`export` with an audio format), voices
  per speaker, `music` for the bed and stings; video podcasts add a still
  or simple picture per speaker with `motion`.
- VertX lowers music and other sound under any voice by itself (ducking).
- Can't do yet: a sound effects library, a loudness target for the whole
  mix.
