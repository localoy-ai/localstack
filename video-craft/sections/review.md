# Reviewing your own cut

Before the person sees it, watch it like a demanding editor would. Most
problems are visible in a few frames; fix them before you report.

## The passes (look at frames across the cut, then the export once)

1. **Story**: is the point clear in the first 3 seconds? Does each scene
   earn its place? Does it end on the ask or the payoff?
2. **Pace**: no still or shot longer than it needs; faster toward the end;
   a breath after a reveal.
3. **Picture**: framing (subject not cut off, nothing important in the
   edges; read every word near a frame's edge: none may be cut), text never
   over faces or the action, colour consistent across
   cuts, no black or frozen frames, no stretched pictures.
4. **Text**: readable on a phone (size, contrast, time on screen), spelled
   right, brand names exact, no claims the sources don't support.
5. **Sound**: voice clear over music, no clipped peaks, no silence gaps
   unless meant, effects on cuts but not on every frame, music ends cleanly.
6. **Brand**: colours, typeface, logo used as the brand does; the
   person's rules (words they never use) followed.
7. **Technical**: right size and length for the channel, safe areas clear
   of the app's buttons, file plays.

## Common faults and fixes

- Words or UI cut by the frame's edge (a wide shot cover-cropped into a
  tall frame: "for the campa…") → crop to the part that matters and punch
  in, or blur fill (`effects.md`, "Wide footage in a tall frame").
- A frame that is mostly empty (blank page, black, a sliver of the
  subject) → crop to the subject or cut the shot.
- A title that whispers (a small label where the brief said bold) → big,
  heavy, over the opening shot rather than alone on black.
- Slow start → cut the first seconds until the hook is the first frame.
- Text gone before it's read → hold it longer or use fewer words.
- Jarring colour jump → match the two shots, or grade the whole video.
- Music fighting the voice → lower it, or pick a sparser part.
- A transition that shows off → make it a cut.
- Everything the same speed → vary shot lengths.

## Scoring before you report

- Note the 1–3 things that would most improve it. Fix what you can
  without new cost; name the rest to the person in one line each.
- Report what was made, where, its length, and what it cost — briefly.

## What this means in VertX

- `look` at 6–8 times across the cut (scene middles, transitions, text);
  fix in one batch; `export`; look once more at any fixed spot.
